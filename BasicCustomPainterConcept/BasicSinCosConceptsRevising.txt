Let’s Check Different Angles

🔹 At 90° (3 o’clock)

cos(π/2) = 0

🔹 At 180° (6 o’clock)
angle = π radians
cos(π) = -1
y = center.dy - textRadius * (-1)
Y zyada ho gaya (neeche chala gaya) ✅
📌 Conclusion:

cos(angle) 1 se -1 tak move karta hai.
Jab cos(angle) = 1 hota hai to y sab se upar hota hai (12 o'clock).
Jab cos(angle) = -1 hota hai to y sab se neeche hota hai (6 o'clock).
Minus isliye ho raha hai takay circle ka orientation correct rahe.

sin(3π/2) = -1

📌 Conclusion:

sin(angle) -1 to 1 tak move karta hai.
Jab sin(angle) = 1 hota hai to x sab se right hota hai (3 o'clock).
Jab sin(angle) = -1 hota hai to x sab se left hota hai (9 o'clock).
Plus isliye ho raha hai taake right side move kare.
Summary
Y minus isliye ho raha hai kyunki Flutter ka Y-axis ulta hota hai (upar negative hota hai, neeche positive).
X ka sign sin(angle) ke basis pe right (positive) aur left (negative) hota hai.
cos(angle) vertical movement control karta hai.
sin(angle) horizontal movement control karta hai.
📌 Final Concept:

sin(θ) left-right movement control karta hai.
